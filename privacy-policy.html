1Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Catalystai Technology Private Limited, operating as Elevate Educations ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you visit this website or submit an enquiry through it.

We are an authorised admissions and counselling partner of SP Jain School of Global Management ("SP Jain"). This website is not the official website of SP Jain. Admission decisions, fees, programme content and degree awards are the sole responsibility of SP Jain and its affiliated institutions.

By using this website or submitting your details, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website or submit your information.

This policy is prepared in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), as applicable.

3Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide to us

When you fill in the "Get Free Counselling" form, request a callback, or contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • City of residence
  • Any additional information you choose to share during counselling, such as your educational qualification, work experience, entrance test scores (CAT / GMAT / GRE / SPJAT), programme preferences and loan requirements
We do not ask for sensitive information such as passwords, bank account details, card numbers or government ID numbers through this website. Please do not share them in forms or messages.

3.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system and screen size
  • Pages viewed, time spent, links clicked and referring website or advertisement
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Campaign information such as the advertisement, keyword or source that brought you to the website (UTM parameters)

This information is collected using cookies, pixels, tags and similar technologies (see Section 7).

4How we use your information

We use your information to:

  1. Respond to your enquiry and provide free admissions counselling about the Master of Global Business (MGB) and other SP Jain programmes.
  2. Contact you by phone call, SMS, WhatsApp and email regarding your enquiry, the application process, eligibility, test requirements, deadlines, interviews, fees and education loan options.
  3. Share your enquiry with SP Jain so that you can be considered for admission and receive information from them.
  4. Help you connect with education loan providers, only where you ask for loan assistance.
  5. Maintain records of our communication with you and improve our counselling quality.
  6. Analyse website traffic, measure the performance of our advertising and improve the website and user experience.
  7. Show you relevant advertisements on platforms such as Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram), and build audiences for such advertising, as described in Section 7.
  8. Detect and prevent fraud, spam, misuse and security incidents.
  9. Comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

We will not use your personal information for a purpose that is materially different from the above without informing you and, where required, obtaining your fresh consent.

5Consent

We process your personal information on the basis of your consent, which you give by submitting the form on this website and ticking the consent box, and for certain legitimate uses permitted by law.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us at the email address in Section 14. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out before withdrawal, and may mean we can no longer provide counselling or admissions assistance to you.

Calls, SMS and WhatsApp

By submitting your mobile number, you authorise us and our representatives to contact you by call, SMS and WhatsApp, even if your number is registered on the National Do Not Disturb (DND) / National Customer Preference Register (NCPR), for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. International transfers

SP Jain operates in Singapore, London and Dubai, so your information may be shared with, stored by or accessed from locations outside India. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled with a level of protection consistent with this policy and applicable law.

7Cookies, pixels and advertising technologies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies, including Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking and remarketing, and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel.

We use them to:

  • Understand how visitors use the website
  • Measure which advertisements and campaigns lead to enquiries
  • Show relevant advertisements to people who have visited our website, on Google, Meta and other sites (remarketing)

Third-party vendors, including Google and Meta, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on your previous visits to this and other websites. When you submit a form, limited information such as your email address or phone number may be shared with these platforms in hashed (encrypted) form for conversion measurement and audience matching.

How to control this

  • You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
  • Google ads personalisation: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Meta ads preferences: https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences
  • Opt out of Google Analytics: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

If you block cookies, some parts of the website may not work properly.

We keep your personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, for example while your enquiry or application is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards (generally up to 24 months from your last interaction) unless the law requires us to keep it longer. After that, we delete or anonymise it.

9How we protect your information

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, such as encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access controls and secure hosting, to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the affected persons and authorities as required by law.

10Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Ask us to delete your information when it is no longer needed

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address in Section 14. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request, and will respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the time required by law.
